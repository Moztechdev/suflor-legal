At Moz Teknoloji ve Tasarım Limited Şirketi ("Suflor", "we", "data controller"), we care about the privacy of your personal data when you use the Suflor mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it and what your rights are, in accordance with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and related legislation.

This document is an English translation provided for your convenience. In the event of any inconsistency between the Turkish and English versions, the Turkish version prevails.

Data We Collect

a) Identity and Account Data: your first name, last name, e-mail address and unique account identifier as received through your Google or Apple account (if you use Apple's "Hide My Email" option at sign-in, the relay e-mail address generated for you by Apple).

b) Content Data: the script/text files you upload to the App, the line and character data derived from those files, and the audio (microphone) and video (camera) recordings you create.

c) Usage Data: your in-app interactions (screens viewed, features used, credit usage history), session durations and error logs.

d) Device and Technical Data: device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, app version, language and region settings.

e) Transaction Data: your credit purchase history (we do not store your payment card details; payments are processed through the App Store/Google Play infrastructure).

f) Data Collected with Permission: when you grant camera and microphone access, image and audio data is processed only during recording sessions that you initiate.

How We Collect Your Data and the Legal Grounds

Your data is collected automatically by electronic means when you download and use the App, create an account, upload content and make recordings. Processing is based on the following legal grounds under Article 5 of the KVKK:

Direct relation to the establishment or performance of a contract (provision of the service),
Your explicit consent (camera/microphone access, optional notifications),
Compliance with our legal obligations (invoice/payment records),
Our legitimate interests (fraud prevention, service security and improvement).

Purposes for Which We Process Your Data

To create and verify your account,
To split the scripts you upload into lines, assign voices to characters and provide the prompter experience,
To process, store and present the recordings you create with your camera/microphone,
To manage your credit balance and carry out purchase transactions,
To ensure the technical operation, security and performance of the App,
To provide customer support,
To fulfil our legal obligations,
(Where you have given explicit consent) to send you product updates and notifications.

AI Processing and Third-Party Service Providers

In splitting your scripts into lines, assigning voices to characters and processing audio/video, we may use authorised third-party artificial intelligence and cloud infrastructure providers ("Sub-processors") alongside our own systems. These providers process your data only on our instructions and for the purpose of delivering the service.

As part of this processing, your data may be transferred to servers located outside Türkiye. Such transfers are carried out with the necessary security and contractual safeguards in accordance with Article 9 of the KVKK and related legislation.

The main categories of third-party services we use are: authentication (Google, Apple), cloud storage and processing infrastructure, artificial intelligence/voice processing providers, in-app payment infrastructure (App Store/Google Play), and analytics and error monitoring tools.

Sharing of Data

Apart from the Sub-processors described above, we share your personal data with third parties only in the following cases:

Where there is a legal obligation (a court order or a request from a competent public authority),
Where you have given your explicit consent,

In the event of a corporate merger or acquisition (users are informed in advance).

We never sell your data to third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention Period

Your data is retained for as long as the purpose of processing requires, subject to the statutory limitation periods set out in the applicable legislation. When you delete your account:

Your profile information and content data (scripts, recordings) are permanently deleted from our systems or anonymised within a reasonable period (no later than 60 days),
Transaction/invoice records that we are legally required to keep may continue to be retained for the period prescribed by the applicable legislation.

Data Security

We apply technical and administrative measures such as encryption, access control and regular security audits to keep your data safe. However, we must note that no transmission of data over the internet and no electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights Under the KVKK

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to:

Learn whether your personal data is being processed,
Request information if it has been processed,
Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose,
Know the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom the data has been transferred,
Request correction if the data has been processed incompletely or inaccurately,
Request erasure or destruction of the data within the conditions set out in the KVKK,
Request that correction/erasure operations be notified to the third parties to whom the data has been transferred,
Object to an outcome to your detriment arising from the analysis of processed data exclusively by automated systems,
Claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

To exercise these rights you may contact us at kvkk.destek@suflor.app. Your requests are answered within 30 days at the latest.

Minors

The App is not intended for users under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from this age group. Users between the ages of 13 and 18 must use the App with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. If we become aware that this has been breached, we will remove the account and the data concerned.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App may use cookie-like technologies (device identifiers, SDKs) for session management, remembering your preferences and usage analytics. You can manage these preferences from your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced through the App. The current policy is always available in the "Privacy Policy" section inside the App.
