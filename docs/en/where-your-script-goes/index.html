Where your script goes

Why other companies are involved

We do not build the AI models that split your script into characters and lines, voice the other characters, or follow what you say in rehearsal. We buy those three services, and the relevant part of your script is sent to those providers to do the work. Saying "we never share it" would be easy and untrue; instead, here is who receives what, and on what terms.

Who receives what

Google (Gemini): the whole document — once to detect its language, then in parts for the analysis itself; all within that one analysis. ElevenLabs: only the text of the lines being voiced — never the whole script, and never your own lines, because you read those. Deepgram: your voice, and only while you are reading your own line; nothing from the microphone is sent while the AI characters speak.

What none of them does: train on your script

None of the three trains a model on your script. With Google we use the paid tier for exactly this reason — on the free tier requests may be used to improve products, and we are not on it. We opted out of Deepgram's model improvement programme; in their own words, data from opted-out requests is retained only for the duration necessary to process the request. With ElevenLabs we turned training use off in the account settings.

Your uploaded file is deleted as soon as it is read

The moment the analysis finishes, the PDF or Word file you uploaded is deleted from our storage — once the lines are rows in a database, nothing needs to read the document again. The full text of your script therefore exists on our storage as a single file only for the few minutes the analysis takes. An hourly job checks that this actually happened.

What we keep, and for how long

The lines stay in your account so we can give you the rehearsal; delete the script and they go with it. On the voice provider's side, request history is still retained under their own policy — the mode that removes it is offered only on enterprise plans, which we are not on. The generated audio clips also stay in a shared cache, so the same line in the same voice is never synthesised twice; they are stored under a key derived from the text and are not linked to your account — which means deleting your script does not delete them. We would rather write these down than leave them out; if the provider or the plan changes, these lines change.

Who on our side can read it

Our admin panel cannot display script text: the script screen carries only counts — how many lines, how many characters, how many are yours. That is not a setting; the panel's data source has no text field in it at all. Our error logs never quote your script either: when a provider fails we store the error code, not the body of its answer.

Where it is stored

The database and file storage are hosted inside the European Union, in Frankfurt. The videos you record are never uploaded to us: they are written straight to your device's gallery, and all we keep is the fact that a take was saved — its length, which script, which character. The video itself exists nowhere but on your phone.

When you want it gone

Deleting a script deletes the uploaded file, the extracted characters and the lines. Deleting your account removes all of it. For the request history held by the voice provider you can send them a deletion request directly; the address is in their own privacy policy.
