Description of the Service

Suflor is a mobile application that allows actors to prepare for and perform audition takes on their own, with the support of artificial intelligence. The App enables users to upload scripts/texts, have those texts split into lines, assign voices to characters, receive line cues (prompter) in audible and visual form, and create their own audition recording using their device's camera and microphone.

Suflor is not a casting agency, a producer or a representation service. The App only provides technical support during rehearsal and recording; it does not guarantee any audition, role or casting outcome.
